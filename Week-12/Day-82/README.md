## 🎯 Learning Objectives

* Investigate a phishing incident from start to finish
* Analyze malicious emails and attachments
* Identify Indicators of Compromise (IOCs)

## 🧠 Investigation Approach

The analysis in this room involves:

1. Reviewing phishing emails
2. Extracting suspicious links and attachments
3. Analyzing domains and IPs
4. Identifying compromise indicators
5. Understanding attacker intent and impact

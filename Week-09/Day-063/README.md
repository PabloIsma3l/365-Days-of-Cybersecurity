## 🧠 Why SOC Metrics Matter

SOC metrics provide:

* Visibility into team performance
* Insight into detection effectiveness
* Measurement of response efficiency
* Evidence for leadership reporting
* Justification for security investments

Without metrics, improvement cannot be measured.

---

## 📊 Key SOC Metrics

### 🔹 Mean Time to Detect (MTTD)

* Measures how quickly a threat is identified
* Lower MTTD reduces attacker dwell time

### 🔹 Mean Time to Respond (MTTR)

* Measures how quickly an incident is contained and remediated
* Lower MTTR reduces operational impact

### 🔹 Mean Time to Contain (MTTC)

* Time taken to isolate affected systems

### 🔹 Alert Volume

* Total number of alerts generated
* Helps evaluate detection rule efficiency

### 🔹 False Positive Rate

* Percentage of alerts incorrectly triggered
* High rates indicate tuning issues

### 🔹 Escalation Rate

* Percentage of alerts escalated to higher tiers
* Reflects triage accuracy

## 🎯 SOC Objectives

A well-functioning SOC aims to:

* Reduce MTTD and MTTR
* Improve detection accuracy
* Reduce alert fatigue
* Enhance collaboration across teams
* Align security operations with business risk tolerance

---

## 🔄 Continuous Improvement Strategies

* Tune detection rules to reduce false positives
* Automate repetitive tasks
* Improve documentation and playbooks
* Conduct regular performance reviews
* Integrate threat intelligence feeds

Metrics should guide decision-making and process optimization.

Strong SOC performance:

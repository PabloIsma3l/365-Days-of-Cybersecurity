## 🛡️ What is a Blue Team?

The **Blue Team** is responsible for:

* Monitoring systems and networks
* Detecting suspicious activity
* Investigating security incidents
* Responding to cyber threats
* Strengthening defensive posture

Their mission is to protect an organisation’s infrastructure, data, and users from attacks.

---

## 🏢 What is a SOC?

A **Security Operations Centre (SOC)** is a centralized unit that:

* Continuously monitors security events
* Analyzes alerts and logs
* Investigates incidents
* Coordinates incident response
* Improves detection capabilities

SOC teams often operate 24/7 to ensure continuous protection.

---

## 🎯 Core SOC Responsibilities

### 🔎 Monitoring & Detection

* Log analysis
* SIEM monitoring
* Alert triage
* Identifying Indicators of Compromise (IOCs)

### 🚨 Incident Response

* Containment
* Eradication
* Recovery
* Post-incident analysis

### 📊 Continuous Improvement

* Tuning detection rules
* Improving response playbooks
* Integrating threat intelligence

---

## 🔐 Why Defensive Security Matters

Defensive security:

* Reduces risk exposure
* Detects breaches early
* Minimizes business impact
* Protects sensitive data
* Supports regulatory compliance

Without defensive security, organisations remain blind to ongoing attacks.

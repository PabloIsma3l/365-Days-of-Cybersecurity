## 🏢 What is MITRE?

MITRE is a non-profit organization that operates federally funded research and development centers (FFRDCs). It works with governments, industry, and academia to improve security, technology, and infrastructure.

In cybersecurity, MITRE provides several open frameworks and resources that help security professionals understand attacker behavior and improve defense strategies.

---

## 🧬 MITRE ATT&CK Framework

The **MITRE ATT&CK (Adversarial Tactics, Techniques, and Common Knowledge)** framework is a globally accessible knowledge base of adversary behavior.

It documents how attackers operate in real-world environments by categorizing their actions into:

* **Tactics** – the attacker’s goal
* **Techniques** – the method used to achieve that goal
* **Sub-techniques** – more specific variations of techniques

Security teams use ATT&CK to better understand, detect, and respond to cyber threats.

---

## ⚔️ ATT&CK Tactics

Tactics represent the **high-level objectives** an attacker wants to achieve during an intrusion.

Examples of common tactics include:

* Initial Access
* Execution
* Persistence
* Privilege Escalation
* Defense Evasion
* Credential Access
* Discovery
* Lateral Movement
* Command and Control
* Exfiltration
* Impact

These tactics describe the different stages of attacker behavior within a compromised environment.

---

## 🔎 ATT&CK Techniques

Techniques describe **how attackers perform actions** to achieve their objectives.

Examples include:

* Phishing
* PowerShell execution
* Credential dumping
* Pass-the-Hash
* Remote services for lateral movement

Each technique is documented with detailed information including detection methods, mitigation strategies, and real-world examples.

---

## 🛡️ How Security Teams Use MITRE

Security professionals use MITRE ATT&CK in multiple areas of cybersecurity:

### SOC Operations

SOC analysts map alerts and attacker behavior to ATT&CK techniques in order to better understand attacks.

### Threat Hunting

Threat hunters search for indicators related to specific ATT&CK techniques within logs and telemetry.

### Detection Engineering

Security teams design detection rules that identify behaviors described in ATT&CK.

### Red Team and Blue Team

Red teams simulate attacker techniques while blue teams develop defenses against them.

## 📊 Other MITRE Resources

In addition to ATT&CK, MITRE provides other useful cybersecurity resources, such as:

* CAPEC (Common Attack Pattern Enumeration and Classification)
* CWE (Common Weakness Enumeration)
* CVE (Common Vulnerabilities and Exposures)

These resources help security teams understand vulnerabilities, attack patterns, and defensive strategies.
